**Status: Available**

Compare who talked to whom, in both directions.

## Try it

Open **Statistics → Conversations** or **Endpoints**. Switch between **Ethernet**, **IPv4**, **IPv6**, **TCP**, **UDP**, and **SCTP** views; sort or search; then use **Show Sessions** to investigate a row.

## What you can see

Conversations show bidirectional frames, bytes, start, duration, and mean bit rate. Endpoints show sent and received totals, related sessions, first observation, and locally known names. The Ethernet, IPv4, and IPv6 views group by network address alone; the TCP, UDP, and SCTP views group by address and port. Optional user-provided GeoIP databases add local enrichment.

## Coverage limits

Totals derive from the sessions in view, so frames with no session may be excluded. Tracexy does not ship or download a GeoIP database.
