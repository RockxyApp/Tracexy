**Status: Partial**

Prioritize observed anomalies and follow each result back to its packets.

## Try it

Open **Statistics → Findings** or select a finding from Overview. Turn on **Group by Kind**, search, switch the severity picker to warnings only, then open a finding’s session and first cited frame.

## What you can see

Selected TCP lifecycle and sequence observations produce bounded findings, including resets, retransmissions, overlap, and out-of-order evidence. A DNS truncated-response bit has a narrow neutral finding. The UI shows severity, count, citations, and coverage limits.

## Coverage limits

This is selected analysis, not a universal expert engine. ICMP and TLS evidence are currently observation-only; missing findings never prove healthy traffic.
