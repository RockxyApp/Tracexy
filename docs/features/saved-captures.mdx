**Status: Available**

Open packet evidence without starting a live capture or installing the helper.

## Try it

Choose **File → Open…** to inspect a PCAP or PCAPNG in place. Use **Import into Library…** when you want a Project-managed copy. Review format and progress before switching to the resulting sessions.

## What you can see

The file header determines format even when the extension is unusual. Gzip and LZ4 wrapped captures and the supported TCP Viewer schema-1 capture payload import into a managed copy. Truncated final records retain accepted earlier evidence with a warning.

## Coverage limits

Zstandard, bzip2, xz, HAR, and arbitrary session archives are unsupported. An invalid or cancelled open leaves the current investigation in place.
