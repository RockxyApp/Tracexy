**Status: Available**

Move from a session-level question to an exact packet.

## Try it

Open a selected session’s **Frames** facet, or **View → All Frames** (⌥⌘A). Search, use **Go to Frame**, apply **Mark Frame**, **Add Frame Comment…**, or **Ignore Frame** from a row’s context menu, and choose a row to open its layers and bytes.

## What you can see

Frame times can be shown relative to a session or capture, as local or UTC time, or from a selected reference. Untimed frames remain untimed. Search and navigation respect the available source and scope.

## Coverage limits

The recent live inspection window is bounded; exact cited frames can be recovered from a stable local spool or saved source. Unsupported layers still expose raw bytes without invented interpretation.
