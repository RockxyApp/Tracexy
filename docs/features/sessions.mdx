**Status: Available**

Investigate conversations instead of manually following a packet list.

## Try it

Open **Sessions**, choose a row, and inspect its endpoints, application hints, timing, volume, and retained evidence. Add columns or sort the table for the question at hand. Open its Evidence facet to follow exact frame citations.

## What you can see

A shared fold groups batch, live, and saved traffic by canonical conversation identity. Optional TCP completeness identifies lifecycle stages actually observed. Process attribution is best effort and remains unknown when unavailable.

## Coverage limits

A session is a view over observed frames, not proof of a complete connection or application exchange. Missing capture time and missing tail packets are shown as gaps in knowledge.
