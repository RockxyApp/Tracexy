**Status: Partial**

Inspect supported call signaling and media stream statistics.

## Try it

Open **Statistics → SIP**, **VoIP Calls**, or **RTP Streams**. Select a call or stream, narrow to its sessions, and open the cited signaling or media frames. Use Stream Analysis for timing, jitter, and loss when present.

## What you can see

Supported views summarize SIP methods and status, call progression, RTP payload type, packet gaps, jitter, delta, skew, and stream candidates. UDP multicast streams have separate rate and burst statistics.

## Coverage limits

Broad telephony decoding, all codecs, audio playback, and every Wireshark telephony tool are unavailable. RTP interpretation depends on observed headers and known clock rates.
