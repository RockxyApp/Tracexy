**Status: Partial**

Inspect supported call signaling and media stream statistics.

## Try it

Open **Statistics → SIP**, **VoIP Calls**, **RTP Streams**, or **UDP Multicast Streams**. Select a call or stream, then use **Show Sessions** to narrow the list, **Open First Frame** to reach the cited signaling or media frame, **Flow Sequence** to see a call's progression, or **Copy Call-ID**. Open a stream's analysis sheet for timing, jitter, and loss when present, as packets or as a graph, and **Save as CSV…** for the result.

## What you can see

Supported views summarize SIP methods and status, call progression, RTP payload type, packet gaps, jitter, delta, skew, and stream candidates. UDP multicast streams have separate rate and burst statistics. A reverse stream is named only when one was observed.

## Coverage limits

Broad telephony decoding, all codecs, audio playback, and every Wireshark telephony tool are unavailable. RTP interpretation depends on observed headers and known clock rates.
