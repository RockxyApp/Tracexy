# Investigate a session

**Overview** highlights hosts, protocols, apps, findings, and traffic activity. Open **Sessions** to inspect a conversation's endpoints, timing, byte counts, protocol observations, and available process attribution. Process ownership depends on capture context and may be unknown.

The bottom **Evidence** inspector shows retained connection and TLS observations. The right **Details** dock provides context for the selection. Open a cited frame to inspect packet layers, payload, and hex. The source file or stopped live spool must still be available for exact byte reads.

**Follow Stream** reads an identity-checked stable source on demand. TCP reconstruction is bounded and reports gaps, overlaps, and truncation. The app also offers supported datagram and application-specific follow views; encrypted TLS/QUIC payloads remain encrypted.
