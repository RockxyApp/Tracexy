# Share the right amount of evidence

**Export Investigation** writes bounded session, finding, and note summaries in supported text formats. Privacy settings can mask IP addresses in those outputs. A protected `.tracexysession` document can omit payload and sensitive decoded fields according to the active Project's settings.

Raw PCAP and PCAPNG exports preserve packet bytes. They are useful for interoperability but are **not redacted** by the protected-session controls. Review their destination and recipients before exporting. Selected frames, time ranges, and supported object/upper-PDU exports are available from the app's File menu with format-specific limits.
