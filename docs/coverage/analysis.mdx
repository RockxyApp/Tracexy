Use this inventory to compare familiar Wireshark workflows with Tracexy. **Available** identifies a supported action; **Partial** identifies narrower coverage; **Coming soon** means unavailable today, with no release date promised.

[Explore the Tracexy feature](/features/statistics) · [All coverage areas](/reference/feature-coverage)

## Conversations and endpoints

**Available**

Session-first tables, host/endpoint projections, app context when observed.

## Follow TCP/UDP/application stream

**Partial**

On-demand bounded follow for stable sources and selected protocols; no universal stream dissector.

## Protocol hierarchy, capture summary, packet lengths

**Available**

Scope-aware Overview and Statistics views.

## I/O graphs, flow graph, timeline

**Partial**

Traffic timeline and supported graphs; not every Wireshark graph calculation or plot.

## TCP stream graphs and deep sequence analysis

**Partial**

Selected lifecycle, retransmission, RTT/flow-control evidence; no full set of Wireshark TCP plots.

## Expert information

**Partial**

Evidence-linked selected findings with explicit coverage limits; not a general expert engine.

## HTTP, DNS, IP, service response, distribution statistics

**Partial**

Selected statistics trees and views over supported decoded traffic.

## Telephony: SIP, RTP, VoIP calls, multicast

**Partial**

Selected SIP/RTP/multicast views; broad telephony analysis, playback, and all codecs remain unavailable.

## Wireless: 802.11, radiotap, WLAN decryption, Bluetooth

**Coming soon**

No broad radio-layer analysis or key management.

## Protocol-specific statistics for the full dissector catalog

**Coming soon**

Coverage is limited to the protocols listed in the app and [protocol reference](/reference/protocols).

## Arbitrary plots, taps, Lua and dissector plugins

**Coming soon**

No compatible plugin/tap execution model.

Wireshark names are used for orientation. Tracexy is a separate, session-centric macOS product.
