Use this inventory to compare familiar Wireshark workflows with Tracexy. **Available** identifies a supported action; **Partial** identifies narrower coverage; **Coming soon** means unavailable today, with no release date promised.

[Explore the Tracexy feature](/reference/protocols) · [All coverage areas](/reference/feature-coverage)

## Ethernet/VLAN, cooked/loopback/raw IP, ARP, IPv4/IPv6, ICMP, TCP/UDP

**Available**

Bounded decode; supported IPv4/IPv6 fragment reassembly.

## DNS, TLS, HTTP/1, STUN, QUIC

**Partial**

Selected metadata and explicit follow views; QUIC long headers only.

## HTTP/2, WebSocket, mail, SSH, local services, tunnels

**Partial**

Selected cleartext and on-demand paths; coverage depends on source and protocol.

## HTTP/3, deep QUIC, encrypted HTTP content

**Coming soon**

No QUIC or TLS decryption.

## Complete Wireshark protocol dissector catalog

**Coming soon**

Tracexy has a deliberately bounded selected set.

## TLS/QUIC decryption and key-log workflows

**Coming soon**

Encrypted application bytes are not exposed.

## Export Objects and upper-layer PDUs

**Partial**

Selected supported protocols and reconstructed objects, with completeness limits.

Wireshark names are used for orientation. Tracexy is a separate, session-centric macOS product.
